After a long time publishing elsewhere, this blog becomes the home of the content I write about software engineering.

## How the blog is organized

- **Articles**: posts in chronological order about what I am studying, testing or learning day to day.
- **[Tracks](/docs/)**: reference content organized by topic ([Java](/docs/java/), [AWS](/docs/aws/), [DevOps](/docs/devops/) and [Observability](/docs/observability/)) that keeps growing and being updated over time.

Use the search box in the menu to find a topic, and switch language or theme (light or dark) whenever you like.

## Where to start

How about testing your knowledge? The [Design Patterns Quiz](https://enoque.dev/design-patterns-quiz/) has questions about the GoF design patterns, with an explanation for every answer.

Suggestions and corrections are welcome: open an [issue in the repository](https://github.com/enoqueleal/enoqueleal.github.io/issues).
